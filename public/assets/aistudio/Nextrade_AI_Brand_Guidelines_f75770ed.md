# NEXTRADE AI — BRAND KIT
Version 1.0 | Website handoff

## Brand decision
The selected direction is the MONOCHROME identity. Treat monochrome as the official brand system.
Do not introduce a colorful logo, gradient logo, metallic effect, 3D logo, outline logo, or alternate symbol.
Use the supplied SVG assets as the logo source files.

## Brand character
- Precise
- Intelligent
- Calm
- Trustworthy
- Modern
- Open and accessible

Nextrade AI is a free and open-source AI financial intelligence platform.
It is not a broker and must not imply guaranteed returns or guaranteed predictions.

## Color system
Core monochrome palette:
- Ink: #080B12
- Black: #000000
- White: #FFFFFF
- Primary text on dark: #F5F7FA
- Secondary text on dark: #929BAA
- Muted border on dark: #273142
- Secondary text on light: #667085
- Light surface: #F7F8FA

Use color sparingly in the product UI for data semantics only:
- Positive: #22C55E
- Negative: #EF4444
- Information: #64748B

These semantic UI colors must never recolor the logo.

## Typography
Preferred:
- Geist Sans or Inter.
- Wordmark artwork is supplied as SVG; do not retype or alter it.
- Headings: bold/semibold, compact tracking.
- Body: regular, highly readable.
- Numeric market data: tabular numerals.

## Clear space and sizing
- Maintain clear space around the logo of at least the height of the symbol's central diagonal stroke.
- Never crowd the logo against navigation, edges, cards, or other marks.
- Keep the full lockup proportionally scaled.
- Do not stretch, skew, rotate, crop, redraw, add shadows, or apply effects.
- For small spaces, use the symbol-only asset rather than shrinking the full lockup.
- Test the symbol at 16px, 24px, 32px, and 48px before using it as a favicon or compact app mark.

## Background rules
- White artwork on dark backgrounds.
- Black artwork on white or very light backgrounds.
- Avoid placing the logo over busy imagery.
- If contrast is insufficient, use a solid monochrome holding area.
- Never place black logo artwork on a dark surface or white artwork on a white surface.

## Website design direction
Premium, modern, minimal fintech. Use a restrained dark-first interface with generous whitespace, precise grid alignment, strong typography, fine borders, and subtle glass surfaces. The logo remains flat monochrome even if the website uses subtle motion or 3D visuals elsewhere.

Avoid generic crypto aesthetics, neon overload, excessive gradients, random floating cards, fake charts, fake market prices, fake testimonials, and unsupported product claims.

## Voice and messaging
Primary descriptor: Financial Intelligence Platform.
Suggested headline: "Market intelligence, powered by AI."
Suggested supporting line: "Research smarter. Understand risk. Explore financial markets with explainable AI intelligence."
Tone: clear, confident, measured, useful. Never promise profit or certainty.

## Legal / trust notes
- State clearly that Nextrade AI is free and open source.
- Do not invent community metrics, integrations, API partnerships, or live-data claims.
- Label demo/sample data clearly.
- Include an appropriate financial-information disclaimer.
- Verify trademark availability before commercial registration or legal claims.
